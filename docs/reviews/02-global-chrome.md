# Global chrome interface review

## Scope and coverage

Scope: the chrome that wraps every route. Root document, locale route, router config, `Nav`,
`Footer`, the three preference switchers, `DocumentNav`, `HomeLink`, `RouteFade`,
`NotOnMacBanner`, `ProtectedBusinessPhone`, plus the theme, i18n, currency, SEO and platform
libraries behind them. Page bodies, `src/components/ui/*` primitives and the `styles.css` token
definitions are out of scope. `styles.css` was read only where the chrome's own classes resolve
(`.nav-link`, `.lang-pop`, `.bs-drawer-item`, `::view-transition`, `theme-wipe`).

Stack: TanStack Start plus TanStack Router, React 19, Tailwind v4, radix-ui, `motion`,
react-i18next. Styling is Tailwind utilities over CSS custom properties (`--washi`, `--sumi`,
`--line`, `--hinomaru`) declared in `src/styles.css`. shadcn-style primitives exist in
`src/components/ui/`, including `select.tsx` (radix Select) and `dialog.tsx` (radix Dialog).
Neither is used by the chrome.

Convention documents found: `CONTEXT.md` (ubiquitous language), `PRODUCT.md` (brand register and
the accessibility bar), `README.md`. No `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CODING_STANDARDS.md` or design-system
doc. `PRODUCT.md` states the bar this surface is measured against: "Use semantic controls, visible
focus states, sufficient contrast in light and dark themes, accessible names for icon controls, and
reduced-motion fallbacks."

Boundary: this is a source review. No browser was used, by instruction. Every claim below is proved
from source. Anything that needs a rendered page is in **Not verified**.

## Verification

Checks run, with results:

1. Skip link. `grep -rniE "skip[- ]?(to|link|nav|content)|#main-content|href=\"#main" src/`. Two hits, both prose comments in `src/components/sections/Pricing.tsx:80` and `:622`. No skip link exists.
2. Main landmark target. `grep -rnE "<main[^>]*id=" src/`. No matches. All 22 `<main>` elements are bare.
3. Inertness. `grep -rn "inert" src/`. No usage as an attribute. Two hits are a code comment and a German translation string.
4. Language of parts. `grep -rn 'lang="ja"' src/`. No matches, against six exposed Japanese strings in the chrome.
5. Listbox keyboard model. `grep -rn "ArrowDown\|ArrowUp\|activedescendant"` over the three switchers. No matches.
6. Theme-switch transition suppression. `grep -rn "transition: none !important" src/styles.css`. One hit at `:2337`, scoped to `[data-reveal]`. The `better-ui` reflow-and-restore recipe is absent.
7. Reduced-motion coverage. Read all 30 `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` blocks in `src/styles.css`. Route fade (`:420-433`), popover enter and exit (`:1451-1457`), burger and close glyphs and drawer rows (`:2478-2490`) and the theme wipe (gated by `no-preference` at `:2298`) are all handled. The drawer sheet and scrim entrance transitions are not.
8. `<html lang>` per locale. `__root.tsx:397` renders `HTML_LANG[isLocale(i18n.language) ? i18n.language : DEFAULT_LOCALE]`, and `I18nProvider.tsx:50-61` re-syncs it on every `languageChanged`. Not hardcoded. No finding.
9. Unique title per route. Read every `head:` in `src/routes/`. Each of the 32 routes sets its own `{ title }`, and `$lang.tsx:38` pulls a localized pair from `localizedSeo`. `__root.tsx:285` supplies the default only. Descriptions are set per route the same way. No finding.
10. Currency persistence. `currency-preference.ts:75-88` writes `localStorage['bs:currency']` and notifies through `useSyncExternalStore`. Locale persistence: `i18n/index.ts:139-144` writes the `bs_locale` cookie for one year. Theme: `theme.ts:43-51` writes `localStorage['bs_theme']`. All three choices persist. No finding.

**Not verified.** Each needs the rendered page. The exact check for the orchestrator:

- Tab into the closed mobile drawer. At 375px width, load `/`, then press Tab from the hamburger and log `document.activeElement` for the next twenty presses. Expected under finding 3: focus enters `#mobile-drawer` descendants while they are invisible.
- Tab out of an open header popover. At 1280px, click the language chip in the header, press Tab once, and log `document.activeElement`. Expected under finding 2: focus lands on the theme chip, not on a list option, while the panel stays open.
- Focus ring on the active nav link. Scroll `/` to the pricing section so `.nav-link[data-active='true']` is set, then Tab to that link and screenshot. Expected under finding 4: no visible change.
- Footer anchor behaviour off-home. Load `/privacy`, click the footer "Get in touch" link, and record the scroll position and URL. Expected under finding 5: URL gains `#contact`, scroll does not move.
- Theme-switch smear. Set `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, switch light to dark, and record at 60fps. Expected under finding 10: chrome elements settle at staggered times over roughly 420ms.
- Measured contrast. Compute the rendered pair for `.nav-link` (`--sumi-soft` on the header's `color-mix` fill) and for the footer colophon at `Footer.tsx:155` (`text-nezumi` at 0.7rem, which is small text and needs 4.5:1), in both themes.
- Focus-ring visibility in dark theme. `focus-visible:ring-sumi/40` is used on nine chrome controls. Confirm the 40% sumi ring is visible against the dark surface.
- Narrow-width overflow. At 320px, confirm the header row (logo plus hamburger) and the footer nav's thirteen wrapping links do not clip or scroll horizontally.

## Verdict

`Block`. Five HIGH findings stand. The cheapest two are also the widest: adding a skip link to
`RootDocument` and deleting `outline: none` from `.nav-link:focus-visible` are each a few lines and
each reaches every route on the site.
