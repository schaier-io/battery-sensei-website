# Glossary

## Plan Names

- **Lifetime**: Canonical one-time plan name. Keep as visible label.
- **Yearly Patron**: Canonical recurring plan name. Replace legacy labels like "Yearly support" or "Ongoing Developer Support" in user-facing copy.

## License Scope

- **Lifetime unlock**: Up to 3 Macs owned by the buyer.
- **Yearly Patron scope**: Up to 5 Macs while subscription is active.

## Tier Name

- **Pro**: Canonical name for the paid tier that Lifetime and Yearly Patron both unlock. Replaces **Premium** in all user-facing copy across every locale. "Premium" is a legacy label; do not introduce it in new copy.
- Relationship: *Pro* is the tier. *Lifetime* and *Yearly Patron* are the two ways to buy it. Both grant the full Pro feature set; they differ only in payment shape and [License Scope](#license-scope).

## Content Surfaces

Three distinct bodies of content. They were previously conflated ("Journal" named the blog while the app had a "Battery Journal" feature); these are the canonical names.

- **Guides**: The written articles about batteries, longevity and macOS. Formerly labelled "Journal" and served from `/blog`.
- **Feature pages**: The one-per-feature reference pages describing what each panel, card and automation does. These are **not** called "guides" — that word now belongs to the articles above. Existing copy that calls them guides ("Fourteen guides, one per feature", "one guide each") is legacy and needs rewriting.
- **Glossary**: Short definitions of battery terminology, one term per page.

## Saga

- **Saga**: Canonical name for the battery history feature in the app — the record of charge cycles and battery events over time. Replaces "Battery Journal" and "battery history" as the visible label.
- "Journal" is retired as a product term. It names neither the articles (now *Guides*) nor the history feature (now *Saga*).
