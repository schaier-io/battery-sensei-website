# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

MacBook owners who want to understand battery health, avoid running out of power, and extend battery lifespan without constant monitoring or technical clutter.

## Product Purpose

Battery Sensei presents battery health, charging, alerts, and history in plain language. The website should make the product feel native, private, calm, and trustworthy while helping visitors understand why it is more useful than macOS and competing battery utilities.

## Brand Personality

Calm, meticulous, and quietly opinionated. The voice is reassuring and human, with Japanese-inspired visual cues used as a coherent identity rather than decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing-page patterns, loud utility dashboards, fear-based battery messaging, technical jargon without context, and decorative Japanese motifs that do not support meaning.

## Design Principles

1. Explain what battery data means, not only what it measures.
2. Make privacy and native macOS behavior tangible.
3. Keep the interface calm while preserving clear hierarchy and decisive calls to action.
4. Use product-specific examples and visuals to show value.
5. Let the Japanese ink-and-paper identity support clarity rather than compete with it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible focus states, sufficient contrast in light and dark themes, accessible names for icon controls, and reduced-motion fallbacks. Do not rely on color alone to communicate comparison results or status.
